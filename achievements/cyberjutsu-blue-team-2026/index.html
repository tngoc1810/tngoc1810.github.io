Achievement Summary An intensive program that immerses students in the operational rhythm of a real SOC. Includes practical exercises on an all-in-one environment with logs, alerts, SIEM, EDR, and a complete investigation flow.

Overview

The CyberJutsu Blue Team course hones analytical capabilities through practical combat scenarios, aiming to push skills beyond the entry-level and into higher-tier SOC roles. It heavily focuses on adapting to the modern era of automation and AI in cybersecurity operations.
